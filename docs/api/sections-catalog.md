## 1. What is on the sheet

`<Sheet id="catalog" tone="ink" page="Catalog" n={2} aria-labelledby="catalog-title">` (cream text on ink, marigold meta bar). One `<h2>` (the outline word CATALOG, solid marigold with a riso offset), then one `<h3>` per band, and one `<h4>` ("Inside the folder") inside an open band.

## 2. The band (accordion)

APG accordion: `<h3><button aria-label="Title, kind, 2022 to 2024" aria-expanded aria-controls id>` (the label replaces the glued visible spans; it adds "No match" and the search hint when they show) + a `role="group"` panel (`aria-labelledby`, `visibility: hidden` while shut, so nothing in it is focusable or read). One band open at a time; first load: band 01 open; clicking the open band shuts it (nothing open is allowed). **Opening never happens on hover.** Keys on the header buttons: ArrowDown / ArrowUp / Home / End move between headers (the stack's `onKeyDown`), Enter / Space toggle (the button's own). Tab order inside the stack: header, then (if open) its OPEN FOLDER link, then the next header.

States (`data-state` on `.pm-cat-band`): `open`, `rest`, `thin` (closed and not matching the current search: a dark tinted **veil** fades in over its body, its tab keeps the folder colour, number and the one-line label turn cream; it is still a button and still openable). Dimming is an opacity change on a pseudo-element: no fill or colour transitions.

* **The colour field** is `<Folder as={motion.div} layoutId={folderLayoutId(slug, 'catalog')}>` in the project colour. `OPEN FOLDER` (a `TabButton` with a real `href={workHref(slug)}`; a plain click runs `openWork(slug, 'catalog')`, modified clicks keep the browser's behaviour) flies that field into the world (docs/api/platform.md section 3). `prefetchWorld(slug)` on hover / focus of the button, `prefetchThumb` on hover / focus of the header.
* **The tab** `FOLDER 03 ->` stands on the top edge at a different x per band (`TAB_X`) and is a pointer shortcut to the same toggle (its hit area is extended to 44 px with a pseudo-element) (the header button is the keyboard / AT control: no second tab stop). The arrow turns down when the band is open.
* **Closed band** (`Motif.tsx`, the "folder stock"): the giant title in the project's own typeface, a **drawing of that project's world** printed tone-on-tone in the band's ink between the title and the meta column (Contract: a spread with picture frames, crop marks, a registration target and a colour strip; Plana: the sunrise in a browser window with its ripples; 44 Lab: a tufted rug with fringe, horns and a tail; Spelled Guilty: a word search with GUILTY circled in pen (the letters are drawn as monoline strokes from a 12 x 14 glyph table, never as <text>, so the motif adds nothing to the heading's accessible name or outline); Space Monkey: stars, an orbit, a satellite, the edge of the Earth; Procreate: a head under construction, hatching, a palette and a pencil; Somia: a shop awning with a hang tag; Gotet: olives, lemon slices, ice and sparkles; the portfolio: a fanned deck of three slides; the Sticker Sheet: die-cut stickers with their paper keyline). `useMotifRoom` measures the room between the end of the longest line of title TEXT (text nodes only: the title's own boxes are block-level) and the meta column and writes `--m-room` (px) on the row: the drawing is as wide as the room, and gone (0) when there is none, so it never fights a long title. The six titles that stand on two lines are broken by the design, not by the browser (`LINES` in `Band.tsx`: Proyecto / Contract, Spelled / Guilty, Space / Monkey, Procreate / Sketchbook, A Portfolio / for a Client, Sticker / Sheet; one `display: block` span per line with a real space between, so the accessible name still reads "Proyecto Contract"). A free wrap would change with the face, and the band would change height when its face swaps in. Then the kind (Fraunces italic 300), `years · group`, a `+` that turns into a cross when open. Plana is set like its logo ("Plana" 800, "Digital" 300).
* **Open band**: dashed rule, the blurb, then a row with the `SlateLabel` (Role / Tools / Group; empty cells are hidden by the primitive) and the **contents slip** `Inside.tsx` ("Inside the folder": three typed lines of what the world lets you do, each with a hand-drawn tick that draws in when the band opens; the template's list of radio-like items kept as a paper object), the CONCEPT stamp + `CONCEPT_LINE` for the three concept folders; on the right the taped print (`Polaroid` 720x900, a `Tape` in the next folder's colour, hand-written caption from `PRINT_CAPTION`, the descriptive alt from `PRINT_ALT`) with the project's die-cut (`peek.webp`, `Cutout.tsx`) stuck on its corner; across the bottom `File 01 · 2022–2024`, the categories as typed paper labels (`Filed under`) and the `OPEN FOLDER` tab button. The slate, the slip and the stamp are re-keyed on every open so the clapper snaps shut again.
* The print, slate, slip and stamp are mounted the first time a band is opened and kept (`opened` set): nothing is fetched for a band nobody opened. The panel folds with `grid-template-rows: 0fr -> 1fr` (the one allowed layout animation: the page below has to follow it).
* **Layouts**: wide (>= 1001 px; on a laptop under 1000 px tall the open band is compacted: print about 29vh, tighter blurb and kit, band about 740 px so OPEN FOLDER and the next tab fit on one 1440x900 screen): text column (blurb + kit) beside the print (from 1600 px the print grows to 28 to 38 rem and the blurb to 46ch, so a 1920 screen has no hole beside the text). Tablet (761 to 1000): blurb beside the print, the kit (slate + slip) runs across the panel under both. Phone (<= 760): print, blurb, slate, slip, concept line, file line, categories, button, in one column; the motif is hidden, the meta wraps under the title and a small taped thumbnail sits beside it. Narrow phone (<= 420): the sheet padding, the film rails, the number column and the title size shrink a little (`--sheet-pad`, `--cat-rail`, `--cat-no`, `--cat-size` on `.pm-cat`) so every title still wraps within two lines at 320 to 430.
* **Touch, coarse pointer, reduced or paused motion**: `inlineThumb` puts a small taped thumbnail in the closed band's header instead of the cursor preview.

### Keeping the visitor oriented (`useHold.ts`, `useReturn.ts`)

* `useHold(still)`: when a band lower in the stack is opened, the open band above it folds shut (up to 700 px). The click handler calls `hold(header, reveal)` first: for the length of the fold (780 ms; 140 ms when still) a frame loop scrolls the page by exactly the distance the clicked header drifts, so it stays under the pointer; when the fold has settled and the band was **opened**, the page scrolls the least that shows the whole band (bottom edge into view, top never under the sticky nav). Any wheel / touch / key / pointer press hands the page back at once. Measured at 1440x900: header at y = 390 before, 390 to 391 during the fold, then the band is brought into view (reduced motion: one correction).
* `useReturn()` (workaround, see section 10): when a world opened with OPEN FOLDER is closed, asserts the remembered scroll position for 900 ms and puts focus back on the OPEN FOLDER link if nothing else has it.

## 3. The ten voices (fonts)

Every band's title is set with `--cat-face` etc. from the rules at the end of `catalog.css` (one rule per `data-slug`): Contract Bitter 800, Plana Sora 800 + 300, 44 Lab Chango (offset tomato shadow), Spelled Guilty Archivo 800 at 85 % width in caps (ink shadow), Space Monkey Orbitron 800 caps tracked, Procreate Gloria Hallelujah, Somia Cormorant 300 (the look-alike for the commercial PP Hatton: never self-host Hatton), Gotet Shrikhand, Concept portfolio Anybody 900 at 125 % width caps, Sticker Sheet Cherry Bomb One with a paper keyline. `--cat-adj` scales each optically.

`faces.ts` `loadCatalogFaces(firstSlug)`: called when the sheet is within about one screen of the viewport (`observeOnce`, `rootMargin: 100% 0`; on a wide screen that is already true at page load, because the cover is short), then it waits for the window's own `load` event and an idle moment, so about 430 KB of display faces never compete with the cover's pictures; idempotent. Per folder it loads **the exact title face** (its own dynamic import: Gotet's title is Shrikhand while its world now uses the brand's own faces, so the catalog cannot rely on `loadWorldFonts` alone) and calls `loadWorldFonts(slug)` (which warms the browser cache for the world, so a folder opens without the film leader). The open band's face first, the other nine when the browser is idle. Fallback until the swap: the display face; closed bands have a `min-height`, and `useMotifRoom` re-measures when fonts finish loading. Measured with every world font blocked (the fallback face) against loaded: no band changes height at 390, 768, 1440 or 1920 (before the explicit line breaks Space Monkey moved 22 px at 390 and Sticker Sheet 102 px at 1920), CLS 0.00007 while arriving from the cover, each font file is requested exactly once.

## 4. Search (index card + engine)

`SearchCard.tsx`: `<form role="search" aria-label="Search the catalog">` with a real `<input type="text" aria-label aria-describedby>`; Courier Prime on ruled paper, taped to the sheet, rotated -1.5deg. The native caret is hidden and replaced by a tomato block caret (class `pm-ambient`: blinks only with full motion, a solid block when still) placed from a hidden measured mirror of the text before the caret. The placeholder is a real element (so the long hint wraps over two typed lines) and is the field's description. Three example searches (`poster`, `magazine`, `logo`) are real buttons. Escape clears a non-empty field; ArrowDown moves to the first match's header; Enter opens the best match, brings it into view (`hold`) and focuses its header. The three example words and the input have a 44 px hit area (negative margins keep the typed line on its ruled line).

`search.ts` (pure, no dependencies): `searchWorks(query, group) -> { words, active, hits, ranked }`, `groupCounts(query)`, `splitMarks(text, words)`.
* The query is cut into words; a folder matches when **every** word is found in some field (substring, accent and case insensitive). A word of one or two letters only counts at the start of a word ("ai" finds the AI tools, not "portrait").
* Fields and weights: title 100, keywords 56, categories 56 (label and slug), kind 38, roles 32, tools 32, group 26, years 26, the three "inside" lines 24, blurb 12. A match at the start of a word keeps all of it, inside a word 45 % ("book" ranks Spelled Guilty above "Procreate Sketchbook"), a whole word +8. Rank: title > keywords / categories > the rest > blurb; ties keep the cabinet's order.
* `hint`: when the match is in a field the closed band does not show (keywords, roles, tools, categories, inside, the blurb) the band prints `Found in keywords: logo`.
* `<Marked>` underlines the matched words with a hand-drawn pen line (three squiggles in rotation, drawn in): never a yellow highlighter. It also marks the three "inside" lines and the categories in the open band.
* Non-matching bands become `thin`. **Typing never opens a band** (a band opening off the screen under a moving stack read as the page jumping): `nextOpen` keeps the open band while it still matches and otherwise folds it shut; Enter or the Down arrow opens the best match. A closed matching band prints its `Found in ...` hint.
* A polite live region (`role="status"`, `aria-live="polite"`) says `N of 10 folders match “q”.` after the visitor stops typing for 450 ms (never on every key); the empty state is read as `CATALOG.empty(q)`.
* Checked in the browser against the real registry: poster, magazine, logo, book, 2023, AI, photoshop, flashlight, cardedeu, "web design", "editorial print", tufting, vermut, an accented word, quotes, empty query, nonsense.

## 5. Preview

`Preview.tsx` `CatalogPreview({ enabled, openSlug })`: a small (160 px wide) taped print that follows the pointer on a spring and leans with its velocity, parked right of and above the pointer (40 px clear of it, `GAP`) so the band's own motif stays readable, while a **mouse** is over a closed band (bands mark the header and the tab with `data-peek="<slug>"`; the component listens on `window`, only while `enabled = pointer fine && !still && sheet near the viewport`). The cursor label "Open" comes from `data-cursor` (the chrome's cursor disc). Rendered in a portal (z 30, under the nav) with `pointer-events: none` and `aria-hidden`. Escape dismisses it until the pointer enters another band (WCAG 1.4.13). Near the top of the screen it swings under the pointer. Thumbnails are mounted the first time their band is hovered.

## 6. Card art

`Thumb.tsx` / `Cutout.tsx` use `public/works/<slug>/thumb.webp` (720x900) and `peek.webp` (sizes in `Cutout.tsx` `PEEK_SIZE`; all ten exist now). A thumbnail sits on a flat colour field in the folder colour with its number behind it, so a missing file leaves a clean field (no broken-image icon, no shift); a missing peek is simply not drawn.

## 7. Motion

Transform, opacity and clip-path only, plus the one layout fold of the accordion (and the title / strip swap and the empty slip, which are the same fold). The stack arrives once (staggered slide-in, hidden state only under `html.js`). Reduced motion and "Pause motion": no arrival, no caret blink, no pen-line draw, no tick draw, no title or motif nudge, no cursor preview (inline thumbnails instead), the hold is a single correction; base.css shortens every remaining transition. Moving things on screen at rest: the caret (a step blink) and nothing else.

## 10. Requests and things other agents should know

* **Router (lead):** when the page URL is `#catalog` (the visitor arrived through the nav) and a world is closed, the browser scrolls to the fragment on top of the router's own restore (a smooth glide to the top of the sheet) and focus is left on `<body>`. `useReturn.ts` works around it for the catalog only; the cause is in `src/lib/router.ts` (`goHomeWithoutLeaving` / `history.back()` land on an anchor entry). Other sections have the same issue.
* **Em dashes:** the lead cut them to two on the whole page (the Contact paragraph and the toast); the catalog's sub and the search placeholder read without one now, and the catalog adds none.
* **`src/lib/works.ts`:** the `concept-portfolio` blurb uses a straight apostrophe ("client's"); the rest of the registry uses typographic ones. The catalog curls it at render time (`curl()` in `Band.tsx`, a straight apostrophe between two letters), so nothing needs to change, but the registry string should be fixed for the Works cards.
* **Dev only:** under reduced or paused motion framer-motion 14 prints one `console.warn` ("You have Reduced Motion enabled on your device...", `warnOnce`, development build only). It comes from the library, not from the sections.
* `CAT_UI` and the `INSIDE` lines could be folded into `copy.ts`; nothing here needs it.
* The catalog needs `loadWorldFonts` to keep its current signature (`(slug) => Promise<void>`) and the title faces to stay installed (`@fontsource/shrikhand` and the ones in `faces.ts`).
