## The numbers (canvas 1200 x 1600 px, transparent background)

Measured on the reference sticker (B_2.png, 735 x 919) and scaled to our canvas. **Corrections to the first brief:** the
black line is ~0.6 % of the height (not 2 %); 2.2 % is the *halo*. Skin, clothes and halo are one and the same flat accent
colour (pixel-sampled: `#C6B1FE` everywhere), the ink is pure black, there is no second tone.

| thing | reference (919 px tall) | **ours (1600 px tall)** |
| --- | --- | --- |
| canvas | 735 x 919 on cream `#FFEFDF` | **1200 x 1600, transparent** (PNG RGBA; SVG without a backdrop) |
| figure height | ink 82.5 %, with halo 86.6 % of the canvas | **die-cut (ink + halo) 88-92 %** = 1410-1470 px, i.e. ink ~84-88 % |
| contour line | 5.5-6 px = 0.6 % | **10 px** (`.k`), round caps and joins |
| interior line (folds, seams, grids, face lines) | 5-5.5 px | **9 px** (`.k2`) |
| detail line (small plaid, ribs, minor creases) | 4.5-5 px | **7 px** (`.k3`, `.k3f`), stitches 6 px dotted (`.st`) |
| smallest allowed shape | - | 20 px wide; gaps between lines >= 14 px |
| halo (die-cut border) | 20 px = 2.2 % | **38 px** (dilate stroke 76), same colour as the fills, rounded, concavities closed |
| halo to canvas edge | 61 px top/bottom, 104 px sides | **>= 60 px** every side (checker enforces it), ink >= 90 px |
| colours | accent + black | `ink #0C0A10`, `accent` (one per sticker), `paper #FFEFDF` (only tiny highlights) |
| ink budget (share of the die-cut) | 22.9 % = 12.1 % solid + 10.7 % lines | total <= 32 %, solid masses 8-18 % (pilot 28.1 % = 12.9 % + 15.2 %) |

Accent per sticker (one hue each, same lightness/chroma family as lilac in OKLCH):

| # | accent | hex |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 01 | lilac | `#C6B1FE` |
| 02 | tangerine | `#FFA65F` |
| 03 | sky | `#85CEF9` |
| 04 | mint | `#8EEBC3` |
| 05 | butter | `#FCE67A` |
| 06 | tomato | `#FA6A57` |
| 07 | pink | `#FF9FC5` |
| 08 | teal | `#66CBC7` |
| 09 | lime | `#C2ED6B` |

Rules for the three colours:
* **accent** fills everything that is body, cloth or prop (skin included) *and* the halo. Parts are told apart by ink lines only.
* **ink** = strokes + solid masses (hair, shoes, belts, hats, speaker cones, lenses). Ink budget, measured on the reference: 22.9 % of the
  die-cut = 12.1 % solid masses + 10.7 % lines. Pilot: 28.1 % = 12.9 % + 15.2 %. The checker enforces total ink <= 32 % and solid masses 8-18 %:
  when a sticker gets too dense, remove lines (not the black masses).
* **paper** = glints, teeth, eye whites, soles, laces, pages, pompoms. A paper shape must be enclosed by ink or sit on ink,
  otherwise it vanishes on the light accents (butter, lime, mint) and on the cream site background.
* No gradients, no shadows, no text, no outlines thinner than 6 px, no ink outside the halo.

## How a sticker is assembled (what `sticker-lib.mjs` writes for you)

```
<svg 1200 x 1600>
  <style>  .fa .fi .fp  fills | .k .k2 .k3 .k3f .st .sh .pp  strokes | .tube-k .tube-a  outlined tube  </style>
  <defs>   every closed shape as <path id>, clipPaths, filters #halo-smooth #wobble #grain, mask #haloMask  </defs>
  <g transform="FIT">                       FIT = translate/scale that centres the die-cut in the safe area
    <g id="halo" filter="url(#halo-smooth)">   all shapes, fill = stroke = accent, stroke-width 76, round joins   </g>
    <g id="art"  filter="url(#wobble)">        parts painted back to front: fill + contour (+ open detail lines)  </g>
  </g>
  <rect filter="url(#grain)" mask="url(#haloMask)" opacity=".16"/>      speckle, only inside the die-cut
</svg>
```

* **Halo recipe**: `feMorphology` is square, so we dilate with geometry: every silhouette shape registered with `shape()` is used
  with a 76 px round stroke, then `feGaussianBlur σ13` + alpha threshold (`slope 18, intercept -7.5`). Convex corners round off,
  gaps narrower than ~50 px (between arm and body, between legs) close up, the edge is clean and anti-aliased. A shape that is
  not registered (or registered with `halo:false`) gets no halo: **every ink pixel must be inside the die-cut** (floating
  doodles such as notes are registered too; they get their own little cloud, merged with the body if they are close).
* **Wobble**: `feTurbulence` fractal noise, `baseFrequency .006`, 1 octave, `feDisplacementMap scale 9`: a slow +-1-2 px undulation
  (wavelength ~165 px) of fills and ink together, so they stay registered and nothing jitters. Give every sticker its own `seed`
  (suggested: 01 = 7, 02 = 11, 03 = 13, 04 = 17, 05 = 19, 06 = 23, 07 = 29, 08 = 31, 09 = 37).
  Do not add a second, finer displacement: it looks ragged, not hand-drawn.
* **Grain**: `feTurbulence baseFrequency .85`, 2 octaves, ink specks at 16 % opacity, masked with the halo shape.
* Fills and contours use the *same* path through `<use>`, so they cannot disagree. Stroke the shoulder end of a sleeve? No:
  use `use(id, 'fa')` (fill only) and stroke its two long edges with open `line()`s so the sleeve melts into the body.

## Workflow (about one hour per sticker once you know the tools)

1. `cp art-src/illustration/sticker-01.build.mjs art-src/illustration/sticker-0N.build.mjs`; keep the imports, the header,
   `createSticker({...accent, seed, title, desc, root})` and `st.write(OUT)`; delete the drawing.
2. **Thumbnail the pose** as a silhouette. It must be readable at 300 px and different from the other eight.
3. **Skeleton**: write down joint coordinates (head centre, shoulders, elbows, hands, hips, knees, feet) in the 1200 x 1600
   canvas. Keep ink inside x 100-1100, y 100-1500.
4. **Garments first**: oversized clothes with crisp straight hems, balloon sleeves, boxy bodies; thin, simple limbs and tiny hands
   coming out of them. `limb(axis, halfWidths)` builds a bent sleeve/trouser leg with a mitred inner corner and a round outer
   corner; its `ends[i].A/B` give you the edge points to start a pattern or a cuff.
5. **Head**: one `headFrame({center, tilt, scale})` shared by hair, face, ear, hat (see *Head blocks* below; or write your own `H(points)`).
   Features go in the lower 60 % of the face, under the hat.
6. **Hands** from `capsule()` (round fingertips!), **props** drawn axis-aligned in their own frame and rotated with a
   `transform`, **patterns** last (plaid = `legPlaid()` in the pilot, stripes, ribs, stitches, hatching, loops).
7. Render: `node tools/render-svg.mjs --in art-src/illustration/sticker-0N.svg --out <scratch>/sticker-0N.png --scale 1 --transparent`
   (env flags of the build script: `FX=0` no filters = fast drafts, `BG=1` paper-coloured backdrop for previews, `GRAIN=0`; render with `--scale 2` and crop with Pillow to inspect details).
8. Fit: `node art-src/illustration/kit/check-sticker.mjs art-src/illustration/sticker-0N.svg` prints the margins; change
   `root: 'translate(x,y) scale(s)'` until the die-cut is 60+ px from every edge and fills 88-92 % of the height.
9. **Quality loop (non-negotiable)**: after each step look at the render next to B_2.png, write down 3 concrete flaws, fix, repeat
   (the pilot took ~16 passes). Then convert: Pillow `im.save(path, 'WEBP', quality=84, method=6)`.

## Head blocks (`blocks.mjs`) - the fastest way to a charming head

```js
import { headFrame, face, ear, features, hairCurly, beret } from './kit/blocks.mjs'
const fr = headFrame({ center: [600, 430], tilt: -6, scale: 1 })   // face-local coords -> canvas (face 224 px wide at scale 1)
group('head', () => {
  face(st, fr)                                                      // accent oval + contour
  ear(st, fr)                                                       // ear with a paper hoop ({ hoop:false, mirrorIt:true } for the right ear)
  features(st, fr, { eyes: 'happy', brows: 'arch', nose: 'small', mouth: 'smile', cheeks: true })
  hairCurly(st, fr)                                                 // FRONT layer; BACK layers (hairBob, ponytail) go before the neck/tee
})
```

* eyes `dot|happy|sleepy`, brows `arch|flat|worry|angry|up|none`, nose `hook|L|small|dot|none`, mouth `grin|smile|smirk|flat|o|tongue`.
* back layer (draw before the body): `hairBob({sides})`, `ponytail`; front layer (after the face): `hairCurly`, `hairCap({bun, part})`, `scrunchie`,
  `beanie({dome:'ink'|'accent'})`, `beret`, `fedora`, `bandana`, `headphones`.
* every block registers its shapes for the halo. Pass `{ id }` when you use a block twice, `{ mouthId }` to features() when two heads share a file.
* Hair and hats are **ink masses** (the reference's black hair): keep them black, add accent shine arcs or knit lines, never strands.

## Building blocks in `doodle-kit.svg`

Use: `<use href="#k-eye-dot" transform="translate(x y) rotate(deg) scale(s)"/>` inside `#art`. Paste only the blocks you need
into your `<defs>` (blocks with a clip carry a `k-clip-*` id). Anchors: face parts = centre of the feature (scale 1 = the pilot's
face, 224 px wide); hands = wrist centre, fingers up, 105 px wide; shoes = ankle (top middle), toe towards +x, sole at y=125,
flip with `scale(-1 1)`; doodles = centre. The catalog reads left to right, top to bottom:

* **face** `eye-dot eye-happy eye-wink eye-sleepy eye-wide brow-arch brow-flat brow-worry brow-angry brow-up cheek` /
  `nose-hook nose-L nose-dot nose-small mouth-grin mouth-smile mouth-smirk mouth-flat mouth-o mouth-tongue` /
  `glasses-round glasses-sun ear` (ear = accent shape with a curl and a paper earring, readable on black hair; mirror it)
* **hands** `hand-open hand-fist hand-point hand-peace hand-thumbsup hand-horns`
* **shoes** `shoe-hightop` (paper sole, laces, eyelets) `shoe-low` (accent swoosh) `shoe-boot` (accent band)
* **doodles** `sparkle star5 heart note1 note2 steam waves speed burst confetti zzz question exclaim swirl patch button`

They are starting points: re-draw them for your pose (fingers curled around a handle, a shoe seen from the front) rather than
bending them. `pen.mjs` has the tools: `smooth` (points -> curve, `'c'` flags a corner), `limb`, `capsule`, `blob`
(imperfect ellipse for dots/wheels), `grid`, `rrect`, `rotPts/trPts/scPts` (move point lists), `spaced`.

## Pitfalls we already hit (so you do not)

* The SVGs are **sources**: class names (`.fa`, `.k` ...) and ids are global when an SVG is inlined into a page, so two inlined stickers would fight. Ship the PNG/WebP (or `<img src="sticker.svg">`, which isolates the file), never inline them.
* Duplicate ids (a group and a shape both called `belt`): `group()` prefixes `g-`. Keep shape ids unique.
* A path with an open outline and a `fill` class paints a black wedge: line classes (`k2`, `k3`, `st`, `sh`, `pp`) are
  `fill:none`; use `k3f` when a filled shape needs a 7 px outline.
* `clip-path` inside a transformed group: define the clipPath in the *local* frame (no `tf`), otherwise the transform is applied twice.
* A `<use>` of a shape that already has a `transform` inside a transformed `<g>` doubles the transform. Transform either the def or the group.
* Fingers built from `limb()` get flat chamfered tips: use `capsule()`.
* Pattern lines clipped by two regions can leave hairline seams: keep lines >= 20 px away from region boundaries.
* Plaid across a sharp bend must change direction at the bend (grid per segment), never bend smoothly through it.
* Ink outside the die-cut (stems of a floating note, an antenna tip): register every ink shape for the halo.
* Do not outline accent shapes that sit on the same accent halo with a hairline: they vanish. Use the 10 px contour.
