- steps (tabs 01–04):
  1. COLLECT — I start by listening and hoarding: the story behind the brand, who it speaks to, what is already on the shelf. Pinterest boards, screenshots, objects on my desk — a reference wall until a one-line promise appears. Chips: Pinterest boards · Reference wall · One-line promise
  2. SKETCH — Pencil first. Dozens of tiny thumbnails on paper, then redrawn in Procreate until a shape, a mascot or a lettering style starts to feel inevitable. Chips: Pencil & paper · Procreate · Thumbnails
  3. MAKE — The sketch gets built properly: vectors in Illustrator, layouts in InDesign, retouching in Photoshop, a quick sculpt in Nomad when a 3D reference helps, and AI tools to explore variations faster — always art-directed and finished by hand. Chips: Illustrator · InDesign · Photoshop · Nomad Sculpt · AI-assisted exploration
  4. DELIVER — Everything leaves tidy: brand manual, print-ready files, a responsive website built in React. A project is only done when the client can run it without me. Chips: Brand manual · Print-ready files · Responsive web
