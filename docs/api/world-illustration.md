# World 10, "Sticker Sheet" (slug `illustration`): API and behaviour note

Folder lilac `#B79CFF`, ink text. Display face Cherry Bomb One (loaded with the chunk by the shell), body face Karla. A sticker sandbox with physics:
nine doodle characters on a cream sticker sheet, an aluminium laptop lid beside it (above the sheet on a phone). Peel, throw, stick, turn, resize,
tip the lid with Gravity (matter-js), arrange, save the lid as a PNG. Concept project: the CONCEPT stamp and `CONCEPT_LINE` come from `WorldFacts`.

Files (all mine): `src/worlds/IllustrationWorld.tsx` (default export, renders and wires) and `src/worlds/illustration/**`.

## Behaviour

* **Peel**: pointer down on a sticker, move more than 5 px (9 px on touch): it lifts (spring `lift`: rises 10 px, scale 1.065, soft shadow grows, 1.6 to 3.2 degree twist, the corner nearest the pointer peels up as a clipped, reflected white flap) and follows the pointer; sideways motion swings it. The sheet keeps a pale die-cut ghost (its own silhouette, dashed cutter line, its number).
* **Throw**: velocity from the last 80 ms of pointer samples; above 240 px/s it glides (exponential friction k = 3.7/s, a little spin, bounces off the lid's play area with restitution 0.46) and sticks flat where it stops. Slower releases just drop. **Slap**: the lift spring is underdamped, so a landing dips under 1 and settles (scale 1.065 to 1, shadow shrinks).
* **Where it ends**: centre over the lid -> stuck on the lid (grows to the lid's scale if it came from the sheet); over the sheet -> snaps into its own ghost; over the desk -> back where it came from (nearest legal spot on the lid, or its ghost). Bring-to-front on pointer down; later stickers lie on top.
* **Turn / resize**: double-click or double-tap +20 degrees (Shift -20), spring with overshoot; `R` / `Shift+R` (15 degrees); two tape buttons on the selected sticker; `Alt` + wheel (0.55 to 1.9); `+` / `-`; size buttons in the tag. A double-click on a sheet sticker peels it onto the lid.
* **Caption tag**: a click without a drag selects the sticker and hangs a paper tag with its caption (Cherry Bomb One), "Details", "Back to sheet" / "Stick on lid", size buttons and close. It hangs above the sticker when there is no room below.
* **Details lightbox**: full image on a field of the sticker's own accent, its alt text, the accent swatch (click copies the hex), previous / next (also arrow keys), Close. Focus trap, `useWorldEscape`, focus returns to the opener, the page behind is `inert`.
* **Gravity** (lever, a tape switch): the lid tips -3.6 degrees and every sticker on it falls, bounces, tumbles and piles up (compound matter-js bodies); drag is disabled while on (click still opens the tag); off lets you pick them up again. The air drag grows with the pile's age so every pile falls asleep (about 2 to 7 s); then no rAF runs.
* **Peel all** (tidy-random pile on the lid, staggered flights), **Arrange** (all nine in a composition), **Reset** (everything flies back into its ghost): tab buttons. They turn Gravity off first.
* **Keyboard**: every sticker is a `<button>`. Tab focuses (shows its tag), `Space` / `Enter` picks up (live region announces), arrows move 12 px (Shift 48), `Space` / `Enter` sticks it down, `R` turns, `+` `-` resize, `D` details, `Esc` puts it back (consumed through `useWorldEscape`, so it never closes the world while something is picked up or a tag is open). Blur sticks a keyboard-held sticker down.
* **Touch**: `touch-action: none` only on the sticker silhouettes (the hit shape is the vinyl outline, so empty canvas corners scroll the page). While dragging near the top or bottom edge of the scroller it auto-scrolls.
* **Reduced / paused motion** (`useWorld().reduced`): no glide (throws just drop), no flights (peel all, arrange, reset jump), no curl, no tilt, Gravity runs the physics to rest synchronously and the stickers jump to the pile.
* **Save my laptop**: renders the lid look + stickers to a canvas at about 2400 px wide and downloads `my-sticker-laptop.png` through a temporary link.
