# Section 05 Process: the darkroom bench (API note)

Source: `src/components/sections/Process.tsx` + `process.css` + `src/components/sections/process/**`. Nothing else in the repo is touched.
Default export `Process` (also a named export), mounted by `App` as the fifth sheet: `<Sheet id="process" tone="ultraviolet" page="Process" n={5}>`.
Dev only (not part of the build, served by the Vite dev server of whoever runs it): `src/components/sections/process/_lab/section.html` renders this section alone (no other agent's code, so another agent's half-edited file cannot break it), `?paused=1` starts it with "Pause motion" on; `_lab/lab.html` shows the four scenes and the seven tokens side by side (`?only=1..4`).

## What it is

An ultraviolet sheet (cutting-mat grid in cream at 5-7 %) with the outline `PROCESS` (cream stroke, marigold misregistration, ghost `(05)`), the sub from `PROCESS.sub`, a hand note, and below it **the bench**:

1. **The film**: a 35 mm strip (ink, `FilmPerforations` top and bottom, edge print `▶ 01 COLLECT` above each frame) running edge to edge of the sheet, with four frames, one per step: COLLECT (marigold), SKETCH (bubblegum), MAKE (tomato), DELIVER (lagoon). A frame = a hand-built SVG scene + the step number, title, paragraph, a hand note and the chips from `PROCESS` (`src/lib/copy.ts`). Frames outside the light are latent: a dimmed tint of the frame's own colour with a band of developer pooled at the foot and a faint ghost of the line work (the active frame is 72% of the film width, so about 14% of each neighbour shows). When a frame becomes the active one it is *developed* once: the colour rises from the bottom like developer in a tray, the objects of the scene land and draw themselves in turn (stroke-dash), the words arrive. A developed frame stays developed (it only settles again, without redrawing, when it comes back into the light). On film wider than 1100px the leader and the tail of the roll are filled like real film: head = a countdown target with the roll number, the taped label `Process`, vertical edge print; tail = a hand stamp `Approved`, the taped label `Delivered`, edge print (all decoration, `aria-hidden`, text in `PROCESS_UI`; they are not `pointer-events:none`, so they count as visible text, and a drag on them is the film's own). Once, 2.8 s after the first frame has developed, the film slides 64px and comes back, as if tugged (an invitation for visitors without a mouse; never in still motion, never after the film has been touched).
2. **The ruler**: a cream ruler whose four segments are the tabs of a tablist (`01 COLLECT ... 04 DELIVER`). A *gate* (one block in the playing frame's colour) slides along it to the active tab; the top edge of each segment is the progress bar: one tick per frame, filled with the frame's colour once developed. Next to it two `TabButton`s: `Back` and `Next step` (both wrap round).
3. **The shelf** (`On my desk`): the seven tools as seven different drawn objects (`--tk` 160 to 240px, growing with the viewport) standing on one plank, each leaning a little, with its name, a two-to-five line honest use and the steps where it is used (the step chips of every token sit on one baseline: `margin-top:auto`). Under the plank, the rest of the desk (`process/marginalia.tsx`, decoration): a numbered ruler (a whole number of centimetres via `round()`), pencil shavings, a coffee ring, and the bin of discarded thumbnails with the hand note `the bin: dozens, then one`.

## Interaction contract

| input | result |
|---|---|
| drag the strip (mouse, touch, pen; the cursor label `Drag` shows over the sprocket rows and `Flip` over a neighbouring frame, never over the text or the picture being read; the ruler tabs have no cursor label) | follows the pointer with momentum and snaps to the nearest frame; the frame nearest to the middle is the active one while dragging. `touch-action: pan-y` and `overscroll-behavior-x: contain`, so the page still scrolls vertically on touch and a sideways swipe is not the browser's back gesture. Verified with real touch events on a 768px tablet |
| a sideways swipe on a trackpad / tilt wheel over the strip | one frame per gesture (no wrap); a vertical wheel and a pinch scroll the page as usual |
| click a neighbouring frame | goes to it (ignored right after a drag) |
| ruler tab click, or `ArrowLeft` `ArrowRight` (wrapping), `Home`, `End` on the focused tab | roving tabindex; the strip springs to the frame, focus follows |
| `Back` / `Next step` | previous / next frame, wrapping |
| hover or focus a tool token | the object plays its own micro-animation (fan opens, pencil writes, tab slides out and the stroke is painted again, clay squashes, tag swings, pin is pressed, key is pressed), a flat marigold spot fills in behind it and a dashed ring appears; the ruler tabs and the frames of the steps that use it light up (marigold number chip + thicker marigold tick on the tab, dashed marigold marquee round the frame) |
| click or tap a tool token | goes to the next step that uses it (wrapping between its steps); on phones a tap only plays the tool (the frames are a long way up the page). The lit state stays about 1.5 s |
| the active step | the tokens used in it stand 12px higher with a dashed marigold ring (the same marquee the lit frames get) |
| phones, `max-width: 720px` | **stack mode**: no strip, no ruler, no buttons; the film is vertical (perforations down both sides), every frame is shown and develops as it scrolls in, the frame crossing the middle of the screen is "active"; the shelf becomes a list |
| reduced motion or "Pause motion" | every frame is developed and fully drawn from the first paint, no springs, no momentum (the strip still drags and moves, and snaps at once), no nudge, no ambient loops, tokens keep one still pose on hover |

The margin notes say only what the layout really does: `drag the film` (mouse), `swipe the film` (touch, strip), `scroll to develop it` (phones, stack); at the shelf `hover a tool and its frames light up` (mouse), `tap a tool and it finds its frame` (touch, strip), `tap a tool and watch it move` (phones).

Semantics: the frames are an ordered list (`ol` named "How I work, step by step"); in strip mode each frame is `role="tabpanel"` labelled by its tab and the ruler is a real `role="tablist"` (`aria-selected`, `aria-controls`, roving `tabindex`; the segments of the ruler are the tabs, clicking a frame selects it too); a visually hidden `role="status"` line announces "Step 2 of 4: SKETCH" when the step changes; each scene is `role="img"` with a real description (`STEP_STYLE[i].alt`); the tokens are real `<button>`s whose accessible name is the tool name + its line + "Used in step(s) ... Press to go to the frame."; the page's single `h1` stays in the Cover: this sheet owns an `h2` (the lettering), an `h3` per step and an `h3` "On my desk". Tab order inside the section: the three sheet-meta buttons, the selected ruler tab, Back, Next step, the seven tools.

Production build (measured with `vite build`): the lazy bench is 40.3 KB JS (11.7 KB gzip) + 29.3 KB CSS (6.3 KB gzip); the main bundle only gets `Process.tsx` and `process.css`. DOM: about 850 nodes, 610 of them SVG. No images, no JSON. Layout shift while the bench arrives (deep link, or a fast scroll to the sheet): CLS 0.0003.

Copy: step titles, paragraphs and chips and the tool names come from `PROCESS` in `src/lib/copy.ts` (the two em dashes of the doc text become a colon and a comma in `stepBody`, so this section has none; if `copy.ts` is ever edited to say that itself, `stepBody` simply does nothing). **Added copy** (not in COPY.md; all in `data.ts`, move it to `copy.ts` if the lead wants one source): the one-line use of each tool (from the step copy and Pau's brief), the four hand notes under the paragraphs (`until one line is left`, `dozens, then one`, `AI explores, I decide`, `done when you don't need me`), the scene alt texts, the label-maker strips inside the scenes (`Thumbnails`, `Procreate`, `Vectors`, `Layouts`, `Retouch`, `Variants`, `Sculpt`, `Print-ready`, `Brand manual`, `Responsive`), `Back to the previous step`, the shelf lead `What I reach for, and where it shows up.`, the margin notes listed above and the roll labels `Process` / `Delivered`. The scenes are vector drawings built in code in Pau's palette (they are not Pau's own pen work, and the page does not say they are).

## Rules the section keeps

- Tokens only (no raw hex in CSS; the scenes use `var(--tomato)` etc.). Animates `transform`, `opacity`, `clip-path` and, inside the SVG, stroke-dash and fill-opacity. No `transition: all`, no colour transitions.
- Every animation has a still path for both `prefers-reduced-motion` and `html[data-motion='paused']` (checked: zero running animations in both); hidden / latent states only under `html.js`; ambient loops carry `pm-ambient` and run only while the bench is on screen and only on the active frame (at most two per scene).
- Nothing under 13 px: measured over every visible text node at 1440, 1920, 768 and 390 (smallest 13px, aria-hidden decoration included); the names written on the tool drawings are 13 user units and the drawings are never rendered under 150px; in-scene strips hide below 270px of picture width (the paragraph says the same things).
- Contrast (WCAG, computed from the tokens): ink on marigold 10.8, bubblegum 8.8, tomato 5.6, lagoon 5.5; cream on ultraviolet 5.5 (muted copy 4.9); marigold hand notes on ultraviolet 3.5 and the pink ones (`--proc-pink`, bubblegum tinted with cream) 3.7, all 24px or larger. The big step numerals are decorative (`aria-hidden`).
- A shelf object is always at least 150px so its name stays readable; on touch devices every tap target in the section is 44px or more (the ruler tabs have a 44px minimum, the `TabButton`s grow to 44px on coarse pointers, the tools are whole 150px objects; the three sheet-meta buttons belong to the Sheet primitive).

## Painted scenes (owner: replace the illustrations, keep the entrance)

The four frames now show Pau's own hand-painted scenes instead of the vector drawings. `process/images.ts` lists the steps that use a picture (`SCENE_IMAGES` = 0..3) and gives the paths `public/process/scene-0N.webp` (1400 x 1500, transparent, drawn on the frame's own colour) and `scene-0N-800.webp` (800 x 857, picked by `srcset`/`sizes`: the 800 px twin on phones and at 1x). They were drawn in ChatGPT as a restyle of the old scenes (prompts PR-01 .. PR-04 in `docs/image-prompts.md`: IMAGE 1 = the old scene for the objects, IMAGE 2 = Pau's poster reference for the hand) and filed by `python tools/ingest_process.py <folder>` (keys the flat panel colour out with a soft edge, which also drops the brush marks of the background and keeps the painted shadows, pads to 14:15, resizes, WebP with alpha).
The vector scenes (`scenes.tsx`), their `art.css` language and the label strips stay in the code as the fallback for a step that is not listed; the label strips are hidden for a listed step (the paintings carry no text). The alt text of each scene was updated to what the paintings show.
**Entrance** (`bench.css`, section "Pau's own hand-painted scene"): `data-dev="0"` the picture is invisible; `data-dev="1"` it rises from the bottom like developer in a tray (`clip-path` + `opacity` + a small `transform`, 1.05 s, once); coming back into the light after being developed it only settles (`data-bump` a/b, two keyframe names so it restarts); a wrapper (`.pm-proc-imgbox`, class `pm-ambient`) idles with a 5px bob while its frame is the active one and the bench is live. Reduced motion and "Pause motion": opaque, still, no clip. Verified (headless Chrome, 1440 and a 390 touch phone): 0 console errors, 0 failed requests, mid-entrance frames show the picture revealed from the bottom with the panel's own developer, the animation names switch develop / settle / none as expected, reduced motion has every animation `none`, the phone stack uses the 800 px twin.

## Tattoo-flash tool icons and bin (owner: replace the seven icons of "On my desk" and the bin, keep their animations)

The seven shelf tokens and the bin are now Pau's old-school blackwork tattoo-flash drawings (four colours: ink, tomato, marigold, cream). Each icon was drawn in ChatGPT as a **parts sheet** (prompts TK-01 .. TK-07 and BIN-01 in `docs/image-prompts.md`; references: the current icon exported from the site + the tattoo poster) and filed by `python tools/ingest_tokens.py <folder>`: the jade working background is keyed out, every sheet is split into its separate pieces (a small piece within 80 px of a bigger one, such as a splatter drop, joins it), the pieces are named by where they sit on the sheet (`name_pieces`; `--list` writes numbered overlays to check) and saved as `public/process/tk/<token>-<part>.webp` (60 percent of the source size, 0.4 MB in all). `tokens.sprites.ts` (generated) holds the pixel size, the holes (the rivet hole of a paint chip) and the anchor points of every piece. The bin is one picture, `public/process/bin.webp`, drawn by `Bin` in `marginalia.tsx` with a CSS drop shadow.
`tokens.tsx` puts the pieces back together on the same 150 x 150 unit board as before: each piece is an absolutely placed `<img class="pm-tk-img">` (`Spr`: board position, width, anchor, tilt) and a wrapper `<span class="pm-tk-g tk-*">` (`G`) is what moves, around a pivot given in board units (`--ox`, `--oy`; one unit is `--u` = `--tk / 150`). **The names the old drawings carried are now real type** on the blank ribbon banner of each icon (`.pm-tk-lab`, label face, 13 px at the least, tighter for names longer than nine characters), aria-hidden: the button keeps its real name. Hover and focus play the same moves as before (`tokens.css`): the fan of three paint chips opens on its rivet, the pencil rides along a scribble that is drawn again (a left-to-right `clip-path` reveal instead of the old stroke-dash), the index tab slides up and the brush stroke is painted again, the clay squashes while the flag waves and the loop tool nudges in, the tag swings from its string and the tick is drawn again, the pin is pressed and the card flutters, the key cap is pressed onto its base and the curly braces pop. Still path for reduced motion and "Pause motion": one still pose (the open fan, the card tilted 4 degrees), no animation, no clip.
**Verified** (headless Chrome, 1440 wide, normal and `reducedMotion: reduce`): all 14 moving parts change as expected on hover (animation names and transforms at 240 ms and 1.4 s), none move with reduced motion, 0 console errors, 0 failed requests; screenshots of every icon at rest and hovered, and of the bin at 1440 and 390. The old vector tokens (`<Svg>` drawings) and the vector bin were removed from the code (the owner chose to replace them for good; `git log` has them).
