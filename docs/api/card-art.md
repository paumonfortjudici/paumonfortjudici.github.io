## Using them
- `thumb`: 4:5, full-bleed art, no text to protect (the folder tab carries the title). `<img width="720" height="900" loading="lazy" decoding="async">`, `object-fit: cover`. Reads at 300 px and 140 px wide (checked).
- `peek`: transparent WebP, upright, <= 640 px tall. Decorative: `alt=""`. Use its own `width`/`height` attributes (aspect ratios differ: 0.67 to 1.21). Display it <= 320 css px tall (2x sharp). Put it BEHIND the folder body and let the body hide its lower part; the recognisable bit is in the top 40 % (table above). Tilt it in CSS (+/-2 to 5 deg) and give it a `drop-shadow`; no shadow or tilt is baked in.
- Every peek carries its own light edge (cream halo or paper edge) and was checked on all seven folder colours, the desk #0e0912, cream and ink (`C:/Users/User/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-User-Documents-Portofolio/0908f779-4789-499a-9f74-ecb97a4d3b2d/scratchpad/shots/cards/peekcheck-all.png`; contact sheets `C:/Users/User/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-User-Documents-Portofolio/0908f779-4789-499a-9f74-ecb97a4d3b2d/scratchpad/shots/cards/contact-sheet.png` and `contact-sheet-desk.png` in the same folder).
- The three concept cards (rows 8 to 10) were checked the same way on all ten folder colours, the desk, cream and ink, with the real CONCEPT stamp simulated over each thumb. Proofs: `C:/Users/User/AppData/Local/Temp/claude/C--Users-User-Documents-Portofolio/0908f779-4789-499a-9f74-ecb97a4d3b2d/scratchpad/shots/cards-concept/` : `peekcheck-concept-brand-concept-portfolio-illustration.png` (the three peeks on every colour), `contact2-cream.png` and `contact2-desk.png` (all ten cards in context, concept thumbs with the stamp), `contact-sheet.png` / `contact-sheet-desk.png` (plain `contact.py`, no stamp), `thumbs-300-and-140.png` and `thumbs-concept-300-140.png` (1:1 at 300 and 140 px wide).
- CONCEPT stamp: `WorkCard` stamps CONCEPT on the bottom-right of the print (right 5 %, bottom 7 %). The three concept thumbs keep that corner (about x 58-95 %, y 84-93 %) on bare paper or flat ground so the stamp stays legible: do not move it elsewhere or recrop the thumbs without checking that window.
- The concept-portfolio peek is a fan of three slides, not one: the two behind lean about -12 and +9 degrees inside the file (the whole peek stays upright and symmetric) and shadows exist only between the cards, nothing outside the silhouette. Because the fan is baked into the file, give it little CSS rotation (+/-3 deg is plenty).
